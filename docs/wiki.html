📖 Realism wiki

Everything in the mod, from the source repo's README. Use the search box to find a block, item or rule.

Real life in Minecraft (NeoForge 1.21.1), built in phases. 1.0.0: everything is in. 1.2.0: surgeons, knives, Czech food, murder evidence.

Phase 1: your body

RuleWhat happens
💧 ThirstDrink or die after 3 days. Water bottles reset it, milk/soup/fruit help a bit. Crouch in water with an empty hand to drink from it. Warnings: thirsty → dizzy → collapsing.
😴 SleepSleep through a night or die after 11 days awake. Warnings: yawning → nodding off → hallucinations (sounds that aren't real).
⚡ StaminaSprinting and hard work drain it, resting refills it. Keep pushing with none left: out of breath → shaky → blackouts → death by exhaustion. Thirst, hunger and cold tire you faster.
💪 Muscle tearsMining or hitting too fast tears your arm (weaker, slower for 2 days). Tear it again = bad tear (6 days, "needs a doctor" in phase 2).
🦴 Broken legsFalls of 7+ blocks can break your leg (12+ always do). Landing on rock hurts more and takes longer to heal.
🔥 BurnsPunching a working furnace bare-handed: 1st, 2nd or 3rd degree burn. A Cold Sweat boiler is always 3rd degree. (In VR, touching it with your hand counts.)
🥶 FrostbiteWith Cold Sweat: freezing for a minute gives frostbite, and it keeps hurting while you stay frozen.

Phase 2: blood 🩸

RuleWhat happens
🩸 BloodYou have 5 liters. Under 4.2 l you're pale (weaker), under 3.5 l dizzy, under 2.6 l you collapse, and under 2 l you bleed out. Blood slowly comes back while you're fed.
🔪 BleedingMob hits, weapons, arrows, cactus and explosions can make you bleed: light (stops by itself), heavy or severe. Breaking a leg on rock is an open fracture: heavy bleeding.
🩹 BandageStops light and heavy bleeding. Paper + string + wool = 3.
🎗️ TourniquetHolds severe bleeding, but you're slower while it's on. String, leather, string over a stick.
🅰️ Blood typesEveryone gets a real blood type (O+, A-, AB+...) by real-world odds, and keeps it after death.
💉 Syringe + blood bagA syringe draws 0.5 l from you or a friend into a blood bag. A blood bag gives 0.5 l, only if the type matches (O- gives to everyone, AB+ takes from everyone); the wrong type makes you very sick.

Blocky pixel blood particles while bleeding: turn them off with bloodParticles = false.

Phase 3: the hospital 🏥

ThingWhat it does
🩺 DoctorA new villager job. Give a villager a Medical Station (white concrete, red dye, iron) and they become a Doctor who sells bandages, burn cream, splints, tourniquets and syringes.
🛏️ Operating TableRight-click it with a Doctor within 8 blocks and 3 emeralds: surgery fixes broken legs, torn muscles, burns and frostbite, stops bleeding and gives back half a liter of blood. You wake up groggy.
🧴 Burn creamHeals 1st and 2nd degree burns; a 3rd degree burn heals twice as fast. Honey bottle + slime ball.
🪵 SplintA broken leg heals 3x faster. Stick, bandage, stick.
🦴 Broken neckFalling 23+ blocks onto rock can break your neck: very slow, no sprinting or jumping, until surgery. Sometimes the spinal cord is hurt too and you're paralysed until surgery. (Like in real life, a broken neck isn't always fatal.)
🩷 Fiberglass insulationPink wall fluff (glass + 2 pink wool = 4). Touch it bare-handed and you itch; break it with no helmet and you cough. It looks like cotton candy. It is not cotton candy.

Phase 4: the mind 🧠

ThingWhat it does
🙂 Mood0-100, you start at 70. Being alone, hungry, thirsty, hurt or out in the dark brings it down. Friends nearby, your pets, music from a jukebox, sunshine, meals and a good night's sleep bring it up.
😔 Low mood / depressionUnder 30 you feel down and move slower. Under 15 it's depression: slower and weaker. It never hurts you by itself, and there's always a way back up.
🎮 Game Console / 📺 TelevisionPlay or watch for +15 mood (once a minute).
🩺 TherapyThe Doctor sells therapy sessions (4 emeralds): +40 mood. In real life too, talking to someone you trust really helps.
📅 CalendarShows the real-life date and the Minecraft day. Paper, paper, red dye, clock.

Phase 5: tech 💻

ThingWhat it does
🛒 Tech DealerA villager job (job block: Tech Counter). Sells RAM, SSDs, GPU anti-sag brackets, motherboards, power supplies, CPUs, GPUs and laptops. GPUs and RAM are expensive: blame the AI.
🖥️ PC CaseRight-click it with each part to install it: motherboard, CPU, RAM, GPU, SSD, power supply. Give it redstone power and right-click with an empty hand to boot. Sneak + right-click switches the operating system.
😬 GPU sagNo anti-sag bracket? The GPU sags, and on the 3rd boot it cracks and is gone.
💾 Operating systemsOriginal look-alikes in the style of a retro Mac, a modern Mac, BeOS, NeXT, retro Windows and Linux (no real logos or artwork). Play a game for +15 mood.
💻 LaptopA computer in your pocket.
🤖 AI Data CenterFeed it RAM, GPUs and CPUs. It eats them all and always wants more.

Phase 6: no reviving 💀

ThingWhat it does
⚫ CollapseInstead of dying you collapse: you lie on the ground and your screen goes black. Nothing can hurt you while you're down: the clock decides. You have 60 seconds (collapseSeconds).
⚡ DefibrillatorHold it, look at the collapsed player (within 3 blocks), press charge → clear → shock (Z, X, C by default; rebind in Controls). Copper, redstone, iron and a lightning rod.
🗄️ AED Wall CaseKeep a defibrillator on the wall: right-click with it to store it, empty hand to grab it.
🚑 Ambulance RadioUse it near a collapsed player: they're rushed to the nearest Operating Table, and if a Doctor is there they get emergency surgery.
💀 Gone for goodNobody saved you in time? You really die and become a spectator (or start over as a new person with permaDeathSpectator = false). The void and /kill skip the collapse.
⚫ Black screenYour screen is pitch black while collapsed, asleep, or under surgery.

Turn it all off with noReviving = false. Back up your world first!

Phase 7: real computers 🖥️

ThingWhat it does
🗂️ AppsEvery computer (PC, laptop, ready-built models) has a desktop with app icons: Notes (saved in the computer), Calculator, Terminal (help, ls, date, whoami, echo, apps, neofetch, clear).
💿 Software DiscsBuy them from the Tech Dealer and right-click a computer to install: Paint (16x16 pixel art, saved), Snake (a score of 3+ lifts your mood), Music (a little keyboard).
🍎 Ready-built lineup13 look-alikes of Macs through the years, with original pixel art and no Apple logo: Compact (1984), Color Compact (1993), Colorful All-in-One (1998), Cube (2000), Lamp (2002), Pro Tower (2006), Slim All-in-One (2012) and Mini Desktops M1 to M6 (the M6 is a "future" model). Every part is already inside; just give it redstone power. They face you when placed.

Phase 8: the world 🗺️

ThingWhat it does
🏛️ Countries/country create <name> founds a country and makes you its leader. /country join, leave, info, list. The leader sets the tax (/country tax <0-50>) and can take money from the treasury (/country withdraw).
🚩 Country FlagOnly the leader can place it. It claims the land around it (5x5 chunks). Walking in shows "Welcome to <country>!"
🏧 ATMRight-click with emeralds: each one becomes 10 money (minus your country's tax). Deposit banknotes, sneak-right-click to take out a 100 banknote, empty hand to see your balance. Your bank account stays after death.
💸 /pay/pay <player> <amount>
📱 PhoneText other online players, see the time, your balance and country. Both of you need a phone and signal. Texts arrive as server messages, so chat filters don't hide them.
📡 Signal TowerPhones work within 160 blocks of one.

0.8.1: hospitals in the world 🏥

Hospital buildings now generate in plains, forests, taiga, savanna, meadows and snowy biomes (only in new chunks). Each has a Doctor, an Operating Table, a Medical Station, 2 hospital beds, an AED wall case with a defibrillator, and a supply chest. Find one with /locate structure realism:hospital. The Ambulance Radio now rushes patients to these hospitals too.

0.8.2: call the ambulance 📱🚑

The phone has a 🚑 Call ambulance button, and there's an /ambulance command (no cheats needed): with a phone and signal, the nearest collapsed player within 256 blocks is rushed to the nearest hospital. The COLLAPSED alarm now includes coordinates. Computers no longer lag on the retro desktops. Dreams and nightmares: while you sleep the black screen shows drifting dreams; you wake up with a dream (+5 mood) or, when you're feeling low or very tired, a nightmare (-5 mood). Turn the sleep screen off with blackScreenWhenSleeping = false.

1.0.0: everything 🌍✨

📜 Eras

The first time an operator opens a world with Realism, they choose where history starts:

  • Year 0: invent everything yourselves. The first time anyone makes an invention, the year jumps to when it was really invented: a book (printing, 1450), a compass (1600), a clock (1700), a Generator (electricity, 1880), a Medical Station (modern medicine, 1900), an Ambulance (1930), a TV (colour TV, 1950), a PC Case (1980), a Phone (2007) and the AI Data Center (2026). /era shows the list.
  • ⚫⚪ Black and white until colour TV is invented (oldTimesVision in the client config).
  • 🎩 Fancy period clothes: everyone starts each life in a top hat, tailcoat, fancy trousers and buckled shoes (wearing the full outfit lifts your mood).
  • 💀 Brutal: before modern medicine you take 1.5x damage, have half as long to be saved when you collapse, and surgery, defibrillators, blood bags and antibiotics don't work yet.
  • 2026: today's world, everything is already invented.

☢️ Radiation and diseases 🦠

ThingWhat happens
☢️ UraniumUranium Ore (deep underground, glows a little) and Raw Uranium are radioactive. The dose adds up in sieverts: 1 Sv sick, 2 Sv weak, 4 Sv hurting, 8 Sv deadly. Away from it your body slowly repairs.
📟 Geiger CounterHold it: it clicks faster the more radiation there is, and shows the dose.
💊 Anti-radiation pillsHalve what you take in for 10 minutes (dried kelp x2 + bottle: iodine comes from seaweed).
🤧 FluCatch it from the freezing cold, rain with no hat, or a coughing friend. Slow and tired for 2 days. Flu medicine (honey bottle + sugar) makes it pass. Never deadly.
🤢 Food poisoningRotten flesh, raw chicken, spider eyes, pufferfish: thirsty and queasy for a day. Water helps.
🌡️ InfectionA wound left bleeding for 2 minutes, zombie bites, fiberglass cuts. A fever that gets worse for 2 days, then sepsis. Antibiotics (bread + brown mushroom + bottle: penicillin comes from mould) or surgery cure it.
🎗️ CancerRare, much likelier after radiation. 4 slow stages (3 days each), you only feel tired. The Medical Scanner finds it: stage 1-2 surgery removes it, later chemotherapy (from the Doctor) often pushes it back a stage. No gross pictures, and there's always treatment.
🩻 Medical ScannerNeeds electricity. A full check-up: blood, radiation, illnesses, cancer.

⚡ Electricity

Generator (100 W, burns coal) and Solar Panel (40 W, daylight), joined by Power Cables. Devices: Electric Lamp (10 W), computers (60 W, redstone still works too), Medical Scanner (50 W), Patient Monitor (20 W). Use too much and the whole grid blacks out. Right-click a generator to see the grid.

🏠 Home

ThingWhat it does
🍳 Grandma's StoveThe old cast-iron stove from grandparents' houses: cooks food 2x fast, warms the room (Cold Sweat), makes everyone near it cosy, and its food is made with love (extra mood). It's hot: touch it and you get burned.
🔨 No crafting tableReal life has no magic crafting box (noCraftingTable). Build a Workbench (2 planks over 2 logs) and hold a Hammer (2 cobblestone + stick) to craft. Every craft wears the hammer down. The 2x2 inventory grid still works.
🎙️ Voice-only chatWith Simple Voice Chat installed, typed chat is off: talk with your voice. Commands and phone texts still work (voiceOnlyChat = false brings typing back).
🥽 VR burnsWith Vivecraft, your real hand touching a burning furnace or boiler burns you.
🔊 EchoSounds echo in big caves and halls (echo in the client config).
🩷 Fiberglass cutsBare-handed fiberglass can cut you: a little bleeding, sometimes an infection.

🚑 The hospital

ThingWhat it does
🚑 AmbulanceA real, drivable van (W/S to drive, A/D to steer). Drive up to a collapsed player and they're loaded in the back by themselves: the siren and lights go on and the paramedics keep them alive 4x longer. Reach an Operating Table with a Doctor and they get emergency surgery. Sneak-punch an empty one to pick it up. The Doctor sells them too.
💓 Patient MonitorShows the nearest patient's heart rate, a moving heart trace (flat when collapsed), blood and oxygen, and beeps with every beat. Needs electricity.
🦾 Chromeflesh surgeryWith the Chromeflesh mod: hold an implant and use the Operating Table with a Doctor (8 emeralds) to have it put in properly, under anesthetic.

⚖️ The law

Countries' leaders are the government. Murder (players or villagers) and theft (opening someone else's chest in a country you're not a citizen of) go on your record by themselves. Leaders use /law setjail, /law arrest <player> (5 minutes per crime), /law release, /law pardon and /law record. In old times only, a jailed murderer can be sentenced with /law sentence: the screen just fades to black, they're gone for good, and their remains stay where they fell (blocky pixel blood only, and only with bloodParticles on). Modern times have no death penalty, like Czechia since 1990. Players who are gone for good leave remains too (remains).

1.2.0: surgeons, knives, Czech food and murder evidence 🩺🔪🇨🇿🕵️

ThingWhat it does
🫳 DraggingSneak + right-click a collapsed player with an empty hand to drag them behind you (again to let go). Right-click an Operating Table while dragging to lay them on it.
🩺 Be the surgeonA Surgery Kit (iron, shears, 2 bandages): use it on a patient at an Operating Table and stay close for 5 seconds. It fixes what a Doctor's surgery fixes and brings a collapsed patient back. Success starts at 60% and every surgery makes you better (up to 98%); a mistake makes them bleed. Hold a Chromeflesh implant in your other hand to put it in.
🔪 KnivesAncient times have swords. From 1900 (modern medicine) swords turn into knives of the same material, keeping enchantments and wear (modernKnives). Knives are quick, often make people bleed, and cut food: knife in one hand, food in the other, right-click. Craft them in the 2x2 grid (material + stick, diagonal).
🇨🇿 Czech foodCooked on Grandma's Stove: houskový knedlík (cut it into slices), svíčková, guláš, řízek, smažený sýr, bramboráky, kulajda, chlebíčky, koláče, buchty, rohlíky and párek v rohlíku, plus cheese and bread slices. Czech home cooking lifts your mood. Dobrou chuť!
🕵️ Murder evidenceAttacking a player is assault; if they die of it, it's murder on the attacker's record, even if they log off. Witnesses within 32 blocks who see it are told (and then it counts even outside any country). Weapons carry the fingerprints of whoever last hurt someone with them. A Forensics Kit on remains shows the cause of death, the weapon and the fingerprints; with a weapon in your other hand it checks it for prints.

1.2.1: JEI 🔍

With JEI installed, crafting recipes (from every mod) show on a Workbench page, with the Workbench and the Hammer as the stations, instead of the crafting table page (which is hidden while noCraftingTable is on). JEI's "+" button fills the Workbench grid for you. Other mods' crafting tables (anything in NeoForge's c:player_workstations/crafting_tables tag) don't open either. Realism also has its own creative tab with every block and item.

3.0.0: Realism 3 🏆

Everything from the 2.0 series in one polished release: the afterlife (judgment, heaven, hell, the dark, lies count, no coming back), daily life (hygiene, bladder, teeth), jobs with paydays, and cars, roads and traffic lights. New in 3.0: the HUD shows 🧼 clean, 🚽 bladder and 🦷 teeth once they matter.

2.0.0: the afterlife ☁️🔥⬛

No more floating ghost when you're gone for good (that isn't real). Instead:

  • Judgment: the screen fades to black and your life is judged, while the camera is stuck in third person (F5 does nothing).
  • Hell 🔥 for murderers and for anyone who did surgery on a patient who didn't agree. A conscious patient says yes by crouching; collapsed patients are emergencies and always fine. Killing someone who attacked you first is self-defense and doesn't count.
  • Heaven ☁️ for anyone who saved a life (defibrillator, surgery on a collapsed patient) or only killed in self-defense, plus a 10% chance for everyone else.
  • The dark ⬛ for everyone else: you see nothing at all, like real death.
  • The record up there sees everything, even crimes outside countries that nobody witnessed.
  • Lying counts too (2.0.1): saying "it wasn't me", "I'm innocent", "nebyl jsem to"... in chat or by phone while you're guilty of something is written down. One lie and heaven is closed to you; three lies and it's hell.
  • Coming back to life isn't real (2.0.1): you stay in the afterlife for good (3.0.1: no command brings you back either). bornAgain = true in the config turns rebirth back on (heaven 10 min, hell 30 min, the dark 15 min, as a new person at the world spawn).

Config section [afterlife]: on/off, heaven chance, minutes in each place, burning in hell. afterlife = false brings back the old spectator ghost.

2.0.2: daily life, jobs, cars 🚿💼🚗

Daily life (dailyLife): you get dirty over about two Minecraft days (faster when sprinting, rain and water wash you) and start to stink (green fumes everyone sees, mood drops): use a Shower, or wash your hands at a Sink. Your bladder fills over about a day: use a Toilet or have an accident. Your teeth go bad over a few days (faster with sweets): hold right-click with a Toothbrush or get toothache.

Jobs (jobs): /job list, /job join <job>, /job, /job quit. Doctor (surgeries, defib saves), chef (cooking food), farmer (grown crops), miner (ores), builder (placing blocks), police (hitting someone with a crime on their record). Payday once a Minecraft day, up to 250, minus your country's tax.

Cars (cars): red, blue, black or white. W/S drive, A/D steer while moving, 2 seats, faster on asphalt. A full tank lasts about 10 minutes: fill a Gas Can at a Gas Pump (20 money) and right-click the car. Crashing into walls fast hurts everyone inside, and running people over hurts them (it's on the driver's record). Sneak-punch an empty car to pick it up. Roads: Asphalt, Road Line, and Traffic Lights (green 8 s, yellow 2 s, red 8 s).

A small panel in the top-left shows water, rest, stamina, blood and injuries (only once they matter). /realism shows your numbers (ops can test with /realism set|bleed|burn). English and Czech.

Config (serverconfig/realism-server.toml): days for thirst/sleep, real-life days instead of Minecraft days, and on/off for each rule.
